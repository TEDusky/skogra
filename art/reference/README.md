## What these say together

- Three to five flat layers, each lighter and closer to the sky colour the further back it is.
- The sky disc (sun or moon) is the brightest thing in the sky. Our clock sits where it would.
- Lights are warm (orange, yellow) against a cool sky (blue, purple). That contrast is the reward.
- The character is the darkest thing on screen, with only the eyes lit.
