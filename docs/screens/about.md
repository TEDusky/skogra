# About

Text only. No settings, no links out of the app.

## Content

- **What it is.** A focus timer. Finish something, and a light comes on in the woods.
- **What it is not.** Not a todo app, not project management. Your work lives where it already lives.
- **Why.** Reward completion, not sitting. Seeing that real effort has visible, lasting value.
